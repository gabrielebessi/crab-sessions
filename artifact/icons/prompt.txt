A sprite sheet of 12 retro 16-bit video game UI icons, SNES / Game Boy Color era pixel art, arranged in a strict grid of 4 columns and 3 rows of equal square cells, with generous empty space between icons. Each icon is chunky low-resolution pixel art drawn on a 16x16 pixel grid and scaled up with hard nearest-neighbor edges: bold dark outline one pixel thick, limited palette of 4 or 5 bright saturated colors per icon, flat cel shading with one highlight, no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no blur. No text, no letters, no numbers, no labels.
Row 1, left to right: (1) a small window frame with a bold arrow pointing out of its top-right corner; (2) two arrows pressing inward on a squashed horizontal block, like a vice compressing it; (3) a pink rubber eraser; (4) a path splitting into two branches like the letter Y, each branch ending in an arrowhead.
Row 2, left to right: (5) a closed wooden treasure chest with metal bands; (6) a closed eye with a diagonal slash across it; (7) an open eye with a blue iris; (8) a bold arrow pointing left.
Row 3, left to right: (9) a green play triangle on a small dark terminal screen; (10) a sheet of paper with a green plus sign and a red minus sign; (11) a version-control branch graph: three round nodes on a vertical line with a second line branching off to one more node; (12) a tiny friendly robot head.
Every icon sits on one flat, uniform, solid pure magenta background (#FF00FF) that fills the whole image, with no shadows, no texture, no vignette and no magenta inside the icons themselves. Each icon is centered in its own cell, all icons the same size and the same style.
